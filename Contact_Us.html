Inquiries and Visitations

Come and partake of the waters of life

✦   ✦   ✦

Hearken unto these words. If thy heart doth desire to know the truth of these things, we entreat thee to travel unto upstate New York. Inquire of the local inhabitants for the location of the Smith family farm in the township of Palmyra or the Peter Whitmer farm in Fayette.

Seek out Joseph Smith, Jun. or any elder of the Church of Christ. They shall expound the scriptures unto thee and baptize thee in the waters for the remission of thy sins.
